Beings on the Land is an index of beings encountered across the Sky Islands of southern Arizona, rendered as embroidered objects. To know another being begins with knowing its name. These hats carry the Latin names of the animals around us as a small act of recognition—and an invitation to look closer.

Made in Tucson and Portal, Arizona. Proceeds support land conservation, restoration, and youth science education. Learn more about how we take care of the land and beings around us.
